# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience. Discuss ideas and observable behavior,
not personal characteristics.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, and
publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the project or its community. Report conduct concerns privately
through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/wangnan0916/ssh-forward/security/advisories/new).
Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept private where possible.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project.
